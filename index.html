FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers.

What is AtlasVault Finance?
AtlasVault Finance is a premier financial institution providing a comprehensive suite of banking, investment, asset management, and risk management products and services tailored to meet your specific needs.
How do I create an account?
Click "Open Account" and complete our simple 4-step registration process. You'll need to provide your personal details, contact information, choose your account type, and set a secure password. The whole process takes less than 5 minutes.
How long does my deposit take to reflect?
Deposits are typically reviewed and credited within 24 hours of submission. You will receive an email and in-app notification once your deposit is approved and credited to your account.
What are the requirements for a business loan?
To apply for a business loan, you'll need to complete KYC verification, have an active account with a positive balance history, and provide the purpose and duration of the loan. Our team reviews applications within 1-3 business days.
How long does an international withdrawal take?
International withdrawals are processed after all verification codes are confirmed. Once approved by our admin team, transfers are typically completed within 1-3 business days depending on the destination country and transfer method.
Can I have more than one account?
Each user can have one primary account with both checking and savings functionality. You can switch between account types or upgrade your account by contacting our support team.
How are my investments protected?
All investments are managed by our experienced financial team. Returns are guaranteed based on the plan you choose, and all invested funds are secured within our platform. Your returns are automatically credited to your account upon maturity.
